AUDIO SETTINGS

Firmware profile

MICROPHONE MODE

Mic 1 and Mic 2 share BCLK GPIO4, WS GPIO5, and DATA GPIO6. Set the microphones’ L/R select pins to opposite levels so one drives LEFT and the other RIGHT. Both stereo is unavailable because this firmware’s processed-audio and Opus transport are mono.

AUDIO QUALITY

The live pipeline currently processes mono PCM16 and sends Opus at the firmware profile rate. Opus does not accept 44.1 kHz input; a working high-rate option needs resampling and a 24-bit capture path, so this choice stays unavailable until the complete pipeline supports it.

DEVICE GAIN

Start listening and connect to adjust microphone gain.

Session-only gain after the ESP32 DSP, before Opus. 100% is 1.0×. Higher settings may clip.

AUDIO PROCESSING

Connect to the selected ESP32 to change microphone mode and DSP controls.

The ESP32 noise gate reduces quiet intervals; it is not AI or spectral noise removal and may soften quiet syllables. Browser GTCRN is the separate AI suppression. VAD reports speech activity without muting. The limiter caps peaks in DSP and before Opus when enabled.
